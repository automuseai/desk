One flat price — read live from the contract

Both lanes pay the same price: pricePerShare(), 0.000841 BNB (≈ $0.50) at launch. This page never hard-codes it — the number above is read from the contract every 30 seconds. Payment must equal exactly shares × pricePerShare(); any other amount reverts, so the contract cannot overcharge you. The owner can adjust the price for future mints with setPrice() — it applies to both lanes equally, and shares already minted are never affected. Max 15 shares per wallet across both lanes combined.

Payment, delivery and closing terms

Payment is the mint: your AutoMuse is delivered in the same transaction — no reservations, no escrow, no claim step. Mint proceeds stay in the contract until the owner withdraws them (visible on-chain). Transfers are locked until the PancakeSwap pool exists and the owner opens trading; that rule applies to every holder equally. The mint is announced to close on 30 November 2026 (the owner closes it on-chain with closeMinting()) or earlier if a lane sells out. Because a mint is a completed on-chain delivery, there is no refund mechanism — check the contract and the exact total before you confirm. No price promises — DYOR.
